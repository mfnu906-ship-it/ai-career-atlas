# New Jersey internship research: IT and fintech business roles

**Research question:** What work and skills do two New Jersey summer-internship postings describe for students pursuing Information Systems-related work, and what portfolio evidence could demonstrate readiness for that work?

**Research method:** I reviewed the two original employer career pages on **November 12, 2026**. The statements below summarize what the employer pages say. Interpretations and the proposed portfolio direction are labeled separately. Job descriptions can change; revisit the original page before relying on its details.

## Original employer descriptions

### Westfield Specialty — 2027 Summer Intern Program, IT Intern

- **Original source:** [Westfield Specialty — IT Intern, Berkeley Heights, NJ (job 12007)](https://fa-exdv-saasfaprod1.fa.ocs.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/en/sites/Careers/job/12007)
- **Source date:** The employer page lists a posting date of September 21, 2026.
- **Reviewed:** November 12, 2026.
- **Location and arrangement:** Berkeley Heights, New Jersey; the description says interns must be in the office four days per week.
- **Program timing and pay:** June 7–August 13, 2027 (10 weeks); $25 per hour.
- **Work described:** Gather and analyze technical requirements; collaborate with cross-functional IT teams to implement solutions; develop digital solutions and test/roll out changes to improve digital-channel user experience; work with software or systems engineering teams to build, code, and automate applications; design and implement network, storage, public-cloud, and private-cloud systems; participate in team meetings and suggest process improvements.
- **Employer-stated qualifications:** Currently pursuing an undergraduate or master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems, Computer Engineering, or another related program; minimum GPA 3.2; communication skills and technical/digital aptitude. Leadership experience is preferred.
- **Deadline:** Not stated in the employer description.
- **Availability at review:** The posting appeared on the employer's career site when checked. This is a dated observation, not a guarantee that applications remain open.

**Evidence-based interpretation:** This is the more directly technical of the two roles. The work verbs include gather, analyze, implement, develop, test, build, code, automate, and design. The employer explicitly names Information Systems as an eligible degree area.

### Broadridge — Summer 2027 Business Internship Program

- **Original source:** [Broadridge — Summer 2027 Business Internship Program (JR1085772)](https://broadridge.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/Careers/job/Broadridge-Summer-2027-Business-Internship-Program_JR1085772)
- **Reviewed:** November 12, 2026.
- **Locations:** Newark, New Jersey; New York, New York; and Edgewood, New York.
- **Work arrangement:** Not stated in the employer description.
- **Program timing and pay:** A 10-week program beginning in June 2027; $25–$35 per hour.
- **Potential business areas:** Business Operations, Finance, Sales, Governance and Risk, Innovation and Strategy, Product Management, and Wealth Management.
- **Work described:** Practical assignments, mentorship and training, a capstone project addressing a real business challenge, and collaboration across teams exploring how data, analytics, and emerging tools (including AI) support innovation. The employer says daily responsibilities vary by assigned team and business area.
- **Employer-stated qualifications:** Currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Business Analytics, Finance, Management, or a similar related field; graduation between Fall 2027 and Spring 2028; GPA of 3.2 or higher; leadership, teamwork, or initiative; exposure to tools such as Excel, Power BI, Tableau, or SQL; ability to interpret data and translate it into business insights; and understanding of generative-AI concepts and prompt-based workflows. The employer also requires applicants to be authorized to work in the United States without current or future visa sponsorship.
- **Deadline:** November 27, 2026. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and may close earlier when positions are filled.
- **Availability at review:** The employer page listed the posting and an application end date when checked. Confirm the live page before applying.

**Evidence-based interpretation:** This role is more business-facing and cross-functional than Westfield's IT internship. Data analysis and communication are explicit, but the exact daily tasks depend on the assigned team. The employer describes the accepted degree field as a “similar related field”; the posting does not specifically name Information Systems.

## Compare the alternatives

Westfield offers clearer hands-on IT responsibilities and explicitly lists Information Systems among qualifying degree programs. Broadridge offers broader exposure to financial technology, business operations, product management, and data-driven business decisions. Broadridge is a possible adjacent fit, but its degree wording is less explicit for Information Systems and its daily duties are less specific.

The postings are evidence of what these two employers describe, not proof that all IT or fintech internships have the same requirements. The listed tools are employer requirements or areas of exposure; they are not evidence that an applicant already has those skills.

## Research-to-portfolio decision

**Finding:** Broadridge asks interns to analyze and interpret data, identify trends, and translate information into business insights; it names Excel, Power BI, Tableau, and SQL as examples of relevant tools.

**Proposed portfolio evidence:** Build one small, clearly scoped data-and-process case study using a non-sensitive sample dataset. Include the business question, a short list of stakeholder needs, a cleaned dataset, one simple dashboard or set of summary tables, and a written explanation of one finding and its possible business implication. This could show both data reasoning and communication without claiming professional experience.

**Planned check — not yet an observed result:** Ask a reviewer to open the Broadridge source, find the data-analysis requirement, and trace it to the proposed case-study evidence. Once the case study exists, have the reviewer reproduce one reported metric from the raw sample data and compare it with the dashboard. Record the actual result only after the calculation and reviewer retry happen.

## Professional direction and privacy

These two postings suggest a useful direction to explore: information systems work that connects technical problem-solving, process improvement, and data-informed business decisions. This is a working direction, not a final career choice.
