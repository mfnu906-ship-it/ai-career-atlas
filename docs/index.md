## The short version

AI teams need people who can build models and infrastructure, turn models into useful products, and bring those systems into real organizations. The names are still settling. A job title is only a clue; the responsibilities tell you what the job is.

This atlas is for students, career changers, and working professionals exploring that landscape. It treats roles as overlapping patterns of work, not fixed occupational boxes.

<div class="role-callout">
  <strong>A useful lens</strong>
  <span>Ask what you will build, who you will work with, where the system will run, and how the team knows it works.</span>
</div>

## A living research project

The AI labor market changes quickly. Each role page distinguishes a working summary from examples in current job descriptions. Postings can close or change without notice; check the linked employer page for the latest version. This is career research, not a claim that every company uses titles the same way.
