# Emerging AI roles: a map

Titles vary between companies, and two people with the same title may do quite different work. The map below groups roles by their center of gravity. In practice, many jobs mix several of them.

| Role pattern | Main question | Typical work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Forward deployed engineer](/roles/forward-deployed-engineer) | How can AI work in this organization’s real workflow? | Discovery, integrations, prototypes, evaluation, production rollout, and learning from deployment |
| [AI engineer](/roles/ai-engineer) | How do we build and operate a useful AI product? | Application architecture, model and tool integration, data flows, testing, product iteration, and operations |
| [AI evaluation engineer](/roles/evaluation-engineer) | How do we know this AI system behaves well enough? | Task design, datasets, scoring, error analysis, human review, and release quality |

## Neighboring titles to investigate

Depending on the company, related work may appear under **applied AI engineer**, **LLM engineer**, **agent engineer**, **AI product engineer**, **AI platform engineer**, **solutions architect**, **AI solutions engineer**, **research engineer**, or **ML engineer**. Use the work and expectations in the description to decide what the role really is.

## Compare the work, not the label

A forward deployed engineer is usually close to customer workflows and deployment environments. An AI engineer is usually closer to the product and its engineering lifecycle. An evaluation engineer focuses on how behavior is measured and improved. These are useful distinctions, not strict boundaries.
